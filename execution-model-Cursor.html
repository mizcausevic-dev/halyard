Halyard run and step states. The product UI is light and dark. This diagram carries the seven-theme set.

What the worker is allowed to do

Run

Step

A condition selects one port. The other branch is skipped and does not run. A delay leaves the step waiting and writes a resume job. The worker does not sleep inside the web request.

If the worker dies after a non-retryable step has started and before the result is committed, recovery marks uncertain_outcome and does not call the destination again. The external action may already have happened. Cancel does not reverse it.
